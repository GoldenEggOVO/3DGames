# Physical tabletop audio

Kenney Casino Audio 1.1, Impact Sounds 1.0 and Interface Sounds 1.0, CC0 1.0.
- https://kenney.nl/assets/casino-audio
- https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds
- https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds
- https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/

Exact archive members, SHA-256 hashes and processing parameters are recorded in `sources.json`, section `physical_audio`. Independent recordings supply the variations; no pitch-shift copies are used. Recordings are downmixed to mono, high-pass filtered, trimmed with a short fade and peak normalized. Ceramic plate impacts provide the stone foley; casino chip contacts provide plastic peg foley. Mahjong sound mappings are recorded in the `mahjong_materials` section. The card-play recording retains its own source notice.
